Hot path

Cache first. Database when it matters.

A redirect is four states, not a query: fresh, stale-while-revalidate, negative cache, or a locked miss. The click is recorded after the 302 has already left.

01

Short links, owned

Custom alias, expiry, QR PNG, and idempotent create. Deactivate without deleting history.

02

Limits that mean something

Sliding window in Lua, per user and per IP. Auth fails closed if Redis is down. Everything else fails open so redirects stay up.

03

Clicks you can audit

Counts, 7/30/90-day trends, recent events. Visitor IPs are masked. No geo theatre — the product reports what it actually stores.
